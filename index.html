Retro Flip Clock

An orange split-flap desk clock, modelled to the millimetre. It shows your local time in 24-hour format.

Scroll to take it apart ↓

01 · Mechanism

A real split flap

Each drum carries a snail cam that winds up over the minute. At the step it snaps, the holdback tine lets go, and the top flap falls 180° onto the card below with a soft, hollow flump.

Turn the sound on (top right) to hear it.

02 · Exploded

Pulled apart

  • Clear acrylic cover and an orange front bezel
  • Flap drums on spindles, with drum gears, snail cams, snap springs and holdback tines
  • Quartz motor driving a nylon reduction gear train
  • Hollow housing and back plate with 2.5 mm walls

03 · Back

Built to come apart

  • Counterbored screws thread into posts in the housing
  • Time-setting knob under the setting door
  • Runs on two AA batteries
