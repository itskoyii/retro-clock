Retro Flip Clock

An orange split-flap desk clock, modelled to the millimetre. It shows your local time in 24-hour format.

Scroll to take it apart ↓

01 · Mechanism

A real split flap

Every minute the top flap falls 180° on its hinge pins, uncovers the next number and lands with a clack.

Turn the sound on (bottom right) to hear it.

02 · Exploded

Pulled apart

  • Clear acrylic cover and an orange front bezel
  • Split-flap movement: flap drums, nylon gear train and a quartz coil
  • Hollow housing and back plate with 2.5 mm walls

03 · Back

Built to come apart

  • Battery door sits flush with a 0.5 mm gap
  • Counterbored screws thread into posts in the housing
  • Runs on two AA batteries in series
